You can't tickle yourself, because your brain spoils the surprise.
When you move your hand, your cerebellum predicts the touch it will cause.
Then that prediction is used to cancel the response to the tickle.
A stranger's touch has no prediction, so it still tickles.
Delay the touch after your move, and it tickles again.
Your brain only mutes the touches it saw coming.
