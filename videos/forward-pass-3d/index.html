A forward pass is one trip through a neural network.
Numbers flow in at the input layer.
Those can be pixels, or words turned into numbers.
Each layer mixes them with weights, then usually applies a nonlinearity.
No learning happens here, the weights stay fixed.
The last layer produces the answer.
