# Shared style for every chapter (1920x1080)
- Background #0b0f14 (or a chapter-specific dark variant of it), text #e8eef5, dim text #8a97a6 (contrast >= 3:1), accent #00e5a0, secondary accent #ffb454 (use sparingly).
- Fonts: one sans for titles/labels (e.g. Inter or system-ui), one mono for code/numbers. Same sizes across chapters: titles 72-96 px, labels 32-44 px, captions 48 px.
- Captions: bottom-centre, y 900-1010, max 2 lines, one caption per sentence, the key word in the accent colour. Keep all other content inside y 90-860 and x 120-1800.
- Motion: ease-out entrances (0.4-0.7 s), no more than 3 things moving at once, a visible visual change at least every 20-30 s, never leave a frame empty or static for more than 4 s.
- Every chapter must use its own visual metaphor (see visual_idea) but this palette and caption style, so the video reads as one piece.
