# The Dress

Topic: why people saw The Dress in different colours. Style: diagram. Angle: the brain guesses the light before you see colour.

## Script
Same photo, two different dresses.
In one survey of 1,401 people, 57 percent saw blue and black, and 30 percent saw white and gold.
Your eyes don't just record colour; scientists think your brain first guesses the light, then subtracts it.
Guess cool daylight, and you see white and gold.
Guess warm indoor light, and you see blue and black.
Same pixels, different guess.
