In one survey of 1,401 people,
57 percent saw blue and black,
and 30 percent saw white and gold.
Your eyes don't just record colour;
scientists think your brain first guesses the light,
then subtracts it.
Guess cool daylight, and you see white and gold.
Guess warm indoor light, and you see blue and black.
