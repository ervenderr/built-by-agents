LEARNING = ADJUST WEIGHTS
A neural network is just layers of simple units.
Every connection between units has a weight.
The signal flows forward, through a hidden layer, to an output.
The output is compared with the right answer.
The gap between them is the error.
Learning means nudging the weights to shrink that error.
Repeat that many times, and the network improves.
